> [!info] Dosage
> Use 2 tbsp. per cup of boiling water, infuse for 10 minutes. Drink a cup or two per day when smoke is bad.

>[!warning] Caution
> - Many sources caution against taking red clover while pregnant or breastfeeding because of hormonal effects.
> - Strain this tea through a cheesecloth to remove mullein hairs and prevent throat irritation.

Quantity: 1.2 oz of loose herb tea

## Ingredients  
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried marshmallow root
- [ ] 0.3 oz - dried goldenrod flowers
- [ ] 0.2 oz - dried plantation leaf
- [ ] 0.1 oz - dried red clover flowers
- [ ] 0.1 oz - dried mullein leaf
---
## Directions
In a large bowl, mix and massage all the herbs together. Transfer to a 32 oz jar and store in a cool, dark place for up to 2 years. 

Makes a good ice tea with honey.
