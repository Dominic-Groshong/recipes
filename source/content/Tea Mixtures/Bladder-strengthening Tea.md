> [!info] Dosage
> Use 1 to 2 tbsp. per cup. Infuse for 10 minutes, covered. Drink 1-2 cups/day.

Quantity: 1.1 ounces of loose-herb tea

#ingredients 
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried marshmallow root
- [ ] 0.2 oz - dried horsetail shoots
- [ ] 0.2 oz - dried agrimony leaf
- [ ] 0.2 oz - dried goldenrod flowers
---
## Directions
Combine, mixing well. Store in a jar for up to 3 years.
