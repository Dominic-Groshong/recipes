> [!info] Dosage
> Use 1 tbsp. per cup, infuse for 10 minutes, covered. Drink 2-3 cups a day at the first sign of cold or flu

Quantity: 1.5 oz of dried herb tea
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 0.6 oz - dried elderflower
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried peppermint leaf
- [ ] 0.4 oz - dried yarrow flowering tops
---
## Directions
Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and mix well. Transfer to a 16 oz jar and store for up to 2 years.
