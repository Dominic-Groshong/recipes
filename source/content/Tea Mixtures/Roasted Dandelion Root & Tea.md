> [!info] Dosage
> Use up to 3 Tsp. of roasted roots per cup of water, simmer for 10 mins and strain. Drink. 

Quantity: 1-2 cups roasted roots
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 6 cups - fresh dandelion roots (washed)
OPTIONAL (for tea)
- [ ] 1-3 Tsp. of dried root per cup
- [ ] Milk (to taste)
- [ ] Honey (to taste)
---
## Directions
Preheat the oven to 300 degrees F, and line baking sheet with parchment paper. 

Use a food processer to pulse the roots until they are roughly chopped (about 1/2 inch long). Spread evenly onto parchment paper, with as much surface area as possible. Roast for 60-90 minutes, checking every 20 mins to turn and ensure they are not burning.

They will turn brown, and smell toasty. Remove from oven and let cool completely, store in a glass jar for up to 2 years.
