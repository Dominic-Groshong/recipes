> [!info] Dosage
> Use 1-2 tbsp. per cup of boiling water, infuse for 10 minutes, cover. Strain and drink warm. Drink 1 cup daily, with honey if desired.

> [!warning] Caution
> Violet leaves contain salicylates, so avoid using them internally if there is a salicylate sensitivity.

Quantity: 1.5 oz of loose herb tea
## Ingredients 
- [ ] ½ oz - dried violet leaf
- [ ] ½ oz - dried cleavers
- [ ] ½ oz - dried red clover
---
## Directions
Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl. Store in jar for up to 1 year.
