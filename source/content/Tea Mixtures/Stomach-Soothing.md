> [!info] Dosage
> Use 1 tbsp per cup. Infuse for 10 minutes, covered. Drink 1-2 cups.

Quantity: 1.5 oz of loose herbal tea

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 0.4 oz - dried marshmallow root
- [ ] 0.3 oz - dried plantain leaf
- [ ] 0.3 oz - dried chamomile flowers
- [ ] 0.25 oz - dried lemon balm
- [ ] 0.2 oz dried licorice root
---
## Directions
Combine all in a bowl, mixing well. Store in a 12 oz jar for up to 2 years.
