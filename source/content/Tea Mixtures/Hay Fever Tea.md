> [!info] Dosage
> Use 1-2 tbsp. per cup. Infuse for 10 minutes, covered. Drink 1-2 cups per day.

Quantity: 

#ingredients 
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried goldenrod flower
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried elderflower
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried nettle leaf
---
## Directions
Ensure all stems are removed from dried plants. Combine using your fingers to break up larger pieces and ensure all ingredients are well mixed.

Store in a 16 oz jar in a cool, dark place for up to 1 year.
