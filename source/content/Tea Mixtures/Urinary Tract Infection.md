> [!info] Dosage
> Use ⅓ cup per quart of water. Infuse for 10 minutes, covered. Drink 1-2 cups per day.

Quantity: 1.5 oz of loose herb tea

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 oz - dried uva ursi leaf
- [ ] 1 oz - dried marshmallow root
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried corn silk
---
## Directions
Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl and mix well. Store in a jar for up to 2 years.
