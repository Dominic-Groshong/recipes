> [!info] Dosage
> Use 1 tbsp. per cup. Infuse for 10 minutes, covered. Drink 2-3 cups a day as needed for cough.

Quantity:  1.5 oz of loose herb

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 0.5 oz - dried wild cherry bark
- [ ] 0.4 oz - dried marshmallow root
- [ ] 0.4 oz - dried thyme
- [ ] 0.2 oz - dried mullein leaves
---
## Directions
Combine all ingredients, mixing well. Store for up to 2 years. 

> [!warning] Caution
> Be sure to strain the tea with a fine mesh or several layers of cheesecloth to remove the mullein hairs, which can irritate the throat.
