Quantity: 8 servings

#ingredients 
- [ ] 4 oz - bacon, diced
- [ ] 1 tbsp - olive oil
- [ ] 4 cups - sweet corn (frozen or fresh)
- [ ] 4 scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced
- [ ] 4 cloves of garlic
- [ ] 2 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
- [ ] 1 tsp - chili powder 
- [ ] 1 (4 oz) can - green chiles
- [ ] Salt to taste
- [ ] 8 oz - cream cheese, in pieces
- [ ] 1/4 cup - mayo
- [ ] 1/4 cup - sour cream
- [ ] 1 lime, halved and juiced
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups - shredded monterey jack, or pepper jack cheese
- [ ] tortilla or corn chips, to serve
---
## Directions
1. Cook bacon until crisp in oven at 400 degrees, on a rack, in a pan
2. Add some bacon grease from oven pan to a skillet
3. Add corn, scallion, garlic, jalapeños, and chili powder. Cook on medium, stirring occasionally, until corn is hot and everything softens (3-6 minutes). Add green chilies and salt, cook for 1 minute.
4. Turn down to low, add cream cheese, mayo, sour cream, and lime juice. Cook until melted (about 2 minutes). Stir in 3/4 of the shredded cheese, until melted. Add in bacon.
5. Add remaining shredded cheese to top, broil in oven until golden brown. Let cool for a few minutes and garnish with scallion greens and chili power on top. Serve warm with chips.
