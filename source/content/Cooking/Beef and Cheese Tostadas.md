Quantity: 6 tostadas

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 roma tomato
- [ ] 1 yellow onion
- [ ] 1 anaheim pepper
- [ ] 1 tsp - hot sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp - southwest spice blend (mexican seasoning)
- [ ] 1 beef stock concentrate
- [ ] ½ cup - mexican cheese blend
- [ ] 1 lime
- [ ] ¼ oz - cilantro
- [ ] 4 tbsp - sour cream
- [ ] 10 oz - ground beef
- [ ] 1 tsp - chili powder
- [ ] 6 flour tortillas (6 inch)
---
## Directions
1. Preheat over to 450 degrees
2. Dice tomato; rough chop cilantro; half and peel onion, thinly slice one half, finely chop remaining until you have 2 tbsp worth; quarter the lime; thinly slice anaheim pepper.
3. Combine tomato, cilantro, chopped onion, juice from half a lime, pinch of salt & pepper to make a "salsa"
4. Combine sour cream and hot sauce, add water 1 tsp at a time until drizzling consistency. Season with salt.
5. Oil pan, add beef, southwest spice, chili powder, and salt. (Taco meat)
6. Once beef is brown, add anaheim, sliced onion, and salt. Cook until veggies are tender; Add stock concentrate and ¼ cup of water. Simmer until thickened, 1-2 minutes. 
7. Drizzle tortillas with 1 tbsp olive oil; brush to coat all over. Arrange on a baking sheet in a single layer, prick each tortilla with a fork all over. Bake on top rack, flip half way through (4-5 minutes per side) until lightly golden.
8. Sprinkle tortillas with cheese blend, top with beef mix, salsa, and crema. Serve with lime wedges
