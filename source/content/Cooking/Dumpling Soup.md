> [!info] Comments
> Recipe has received the best praise I've gotten, and is the only meal where I've been asked for my recipe. 

Quantity: A pots worth

#ingredients 
- [ ] 1 large sweet onion
- [ ] 4 large carrots
- [ ] 6 med-large yellow potato's
- [ ] 1 bundle of celery 
- [ ] 3 tbsp of wash-your-sister sauce (warschauer)
- [ ] 2 tbsp of parsley 
- [ ] 2 tbsp of black pepper
- [ ] 1-3 cups - meat of choice 
- [ ] 16 oz can - cream of chicken
- [ ] 32oz of chicken stock (minimum), plus however much feels right once all the veggies, meat, milk, and heavy cream are added.
- [ ] ~1 cup of whole milk
- [ ] ~1 cup of heavy cream
- [ ] 1 can of large buttermilk biscuits
---
## Directions
1. Chop celery, carrots, and onion, cook until soft with olive oil [^1] and wash-your-sister sauce. Once soft, add everything but the biscuits. 
2. Cook for 20 minutes on a slow boil until potato's are fork tender. 
3. Cut biscuits into thirds and add to slow boiling pot, put on lid and let steam for 20 mins. Serve.

[^1]: Original recipe used one stick of butter.
