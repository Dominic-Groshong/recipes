Quantity: 2

#ingredients 
- [ ] 600 g - potatoes
- [ ] 2 tbsp - butter
- [ ] 5 tbsp - flour
- [ ] 60 g - mozzarella
- [ ] 30 g - chorizo
- [ ] 5 ml - olive oil
---
## Directions
1. Peel and boil potatoes until soft, then drain and mash
2. Add the butter and flour, mix with a fork and "kneed" until smooth. Form a ball with your hands.
3. Lay out a sheet of cling film, drizzle olive oil over it.
4. Add potato ball to the cling film and press to make large circle.
5. Add the mozzarella and chorizo to the center of the circle.
6. Use the cling film to roll the potato over the inner ingredients sealing any gaps.
7. Air fry at 450 F for 7-8 minutes on each side.
8. Serve.
