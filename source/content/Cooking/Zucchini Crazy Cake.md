Quantity: 8x13 cake

#ingredients 
**Wet**
- [ ] 1/3 cup - oil
- [ ] 2 tsp. white or apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 2 tsp. vanilla
- [ ] 2 cups - water
- [ ] 3 cups - shredded zucchini (squeeze out as much water as possible using cheesecloth or a thin kitchen towel)
**Dry**
- [ ] 3 cups 4 tbsp. - flour
- [ ] 2 cups - sugar
- [ ] 2 tsp. - baking soda
- [ ] 1 tsp. - salt
- [ ] 1/2 cup - coca powder

---
## Directions
Preheat oven to 350 degrees.

Wisk together all dry ingredients. Add all the wet and zucchini. Mix well, pour into sprayed 8x13 inch casserole pan. Bake 40-45 minutes. Insert a toothpick to check if done. Cover with a [[Chocolate Ganache|chocolate ganache]].
