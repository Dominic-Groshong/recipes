#ingredients 
- [ ] 4 1/2 pounds - hawthorn berries
- [ ] 1 quart - apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp. - sea salt
- [ ] Sprigs of fresh thyme
- [ ] 3 cups - organic light-brown sugar
- [ ] 2 tbsp. - ground ginger
- [ ] 1 tbsp. - ground nutmeg
- [ ] 1/2 tbsp. - ground cloves
- [ ] 1/2 tbsp. - ground allspice
- [ ] 1/2 tsp. - ground black pepper
---
## Directions
Add berries to 6 quart pot, add vinegar and salt, bring to a boil. Once boiling, turn down the heat to a simmer, cover the pot and cook the berries until soft (about 45 minutes). 

Pass the cooked berries through a food mill to remove seeds. Should get about 2 pints of pulp.

Rinse out the 6 quart pot and add the hawthorn paste back into it. Remove the leaves from the sprigs of thyme and add to the pot along with sugar and spices. Cook for 10 minutes on medium heat until sugar is dissolved, stirring constantly to prevent sugar from burning. 

Store for immediate use, or freeze to store for up to 6 months, canning the chutney using a water bath is also a good option for storage.
