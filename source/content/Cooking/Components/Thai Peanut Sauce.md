Quantity:  ¾ cup

## Ingredients 
- [ ] ½ cup - peanut butter (any kind)
- [ ] 2 tbsp - lime juice
- [ ] 1 tbsp - soy sauce
- [ ] 1-2 tsp - chili sauce (sambal oelek or sriracha)
- [ ] 1 tsp - brown/granulated sugar
- [ ] 1 clove of garlic, grated
---
## Directions
In a medium bowl, whisk together all ingredients. Add warm water, 1 tbsp at a time, until you've reached desired consistency. Thicker for dipping sauce, thinner for salad dressing. Keeps for a week in the fridge.
