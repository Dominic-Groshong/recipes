> [!info] Details
> Add 1/4 a cup of syrup to ice water and soda water to finish as a beverage.

Quantity: 

#ingredients 
- [ ] 2 cups - fresh blue or red elderflowers
- [ ] 1 small lemon, sliced
- [ ] 1 cup - granulated sugar
- [ ] 1 cup - water
- [ ] 1 tbsp - vodka or brandy (optional)
---
## Directions
Remove most stems from elderflowers. Loosely pack into a 16 oz jar with lemon slices. Combine sugar and water in a medium sauce pan on medium heat until sugar is dissolved. Pour the hot simple syrup over the elderflowers and let sit for 4-6 hours with the lid resting on top of jar. 

Strain the syrup using cheesecloth or fine-mesh, add the vodka or brandy. Store in a glass bottle for 1-6 months. Dispose once you see signs of spoiling (mold).
