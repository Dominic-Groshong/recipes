## Ingredients 
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 tsp - dijon mustard
- [ ] 2 tbsp - lemon juice
- [ ] 1 clove - garlic
- [ ] 2 cups - vegetable oil
---
## Directions
1. Combine eggs, dijon, lemon juice and garlic. 
2. Mix using immersion blender. 
3. Slowly add oil over the top of mixture.
4. Slowly pulse blender on and off, raising the blender
