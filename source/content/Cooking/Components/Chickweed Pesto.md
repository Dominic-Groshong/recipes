Quantity: about 1 cup

#ingredients 
- [ ] 1/2 cup - toasted sunflower seeds
- [ ] 4 cups - tightly packed fresh chickweed
- [ ] 1/4 cup - fresh lemon juice (1 large lemon)
- [ ] 1 clove garlic [^1]
- [ ] 1/4 Tsp. - sea salt
- [ ] 1/3 cup - extra-virgin olive oil
---
## Directions
Put the seeds into a food processer, and grind into a meal. Add the chickweed, lemon, garlic, and salt and process into a paste (about 1 minute). Add the oil and pulse to combine. 

Seal into container in the fridge for up to 5 days. Partition into a silicon ice cube tray and freeze for 24-48 hours, once frozen remove from tray and place in zip lock bag. Store in the freezer for up to 3 years. 

[^1]: Add garlic until your ancestors tell you to stop.
