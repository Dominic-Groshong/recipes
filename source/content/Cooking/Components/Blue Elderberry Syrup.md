> [!info] Details
> More a food item then medicinal but is a natural anti-inflammatory. Take by the spoonful during flu and cold season. Drink with soda water or use as a syrup over pancakes. 

Quantity: 

#ingredients 
- [ ] 3 cups - water
- [ ] 1 cup - fresh blue elderberries or 1/2 cup dried.
- [ ] 1/2 inch - fresh ginger, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 cinnamon stick
- [ ] 1-1 1/2  cups - sugar
- [ ] 1 tbsp. - brandy or vodka (optional)
---
## Directions
Add water, elderberries, ginger, and cinnamon to a medium sucepan and bring to a boil over high heat. Turn the heat to low and let simmer for 30 mins, or until liquid has reduced by half.

Strain through fine mesh into a glass measuring cup. Add the same volume of sugar as the liquid. Add brandy or vodka. Will last 1-6 months in the fridge, once signs of spoiling discard.
