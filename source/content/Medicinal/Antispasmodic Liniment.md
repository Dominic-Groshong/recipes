> [!info] Dosage
> Apply to the affected area several times a day, rubbing vigorously with your hands to help it absorb into the skin. Apply a hot towel over it for even greater effects.

> [!Warning] Caution 
> Large doses of cramp bark may cause nausea and vomitting.

Quantity: 3 oz

#ingredients 
- [ ] 1 fluid oz - [[_Tincture chart|cramp bark tincture]]

- [ ] 1 fluid oz - lobelia tincture

## Directions
Mix tinctures together in a amber glass bottle with a spray or roller topper. Will keep for up to 5 years.
