> [!info] Dosage
> Spray the affected area several time a day as needed. 

>[!warning] Cautions
> Like asprin, willow bark thins the blood, so do not take it with any blood-thinning medications.

Put this in a spray bottle and apply to sunburns and after any type of hair removal that can irritate hair follicles and leave redness. It's soothing, astringent, and anti-inflammatory. Follow with plantain infused oil. Uses a 12 oz bottle of store bought witch hazel, a liquid astringent used for first aid and skin care.

Quantity: 11 oz
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 ½ cups - packed fresh willow bark
- [ ] ½ cup - fresh or dried calendula flowers
- [ ] 12 fluid oz - witch hazel
---
## Directions
Loosely pack the bark and flowers into a 16 oz jar. Pour the witch hazel over hte herbs to cover. Let sit in a cool, dark place for 4 weeks, then strain out the plant material using cheesecloth. 

Store in a plastic, glass, ore metal spray bottle for up to 1 year.
