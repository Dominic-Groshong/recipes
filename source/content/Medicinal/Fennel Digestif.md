#ingredients 
- [ ] 2 tbsp. - fresh or dried fennel seeds
- [ ] 4 tbsp. - sugar
- [ ] 2 tsp. - orange zest
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups - 80 proof alcohol (vodka)
---
## Directions
If using fresh fennel seeds, crush them with mortar and pestle before adding to a 16 oz jar. Add sugar and orange zest and pour vodka over the top. Seal the jar and shake vigorously until all sugar is dissolved. Let sit in a cool, dark place for four weeks.  Strain using a cheesecloth and store for up to 5 years.
