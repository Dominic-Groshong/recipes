> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops 2-3 times a day.

Quantity: 8 oz

#ingredients 
- [ ] 2 cups - fresh hawthorn flowers
- [ ] 1 cup - 100 proof vodka (more as needed)
---
## Directions
Loosely pack a 16 oz jar with flowers, add vodka until it reaches the level of the flowers. Seal the jar with a lid and label. Let it sit in a cool, dark place for 1 month, shaking occasionally. Strain through a cheese cloth and store for up to 5 years.
