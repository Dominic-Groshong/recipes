> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops every 1-2 hours while the pain persists.

>[!warning] Cautions
> Like asprin, willow bark thins the blood, so do not take it with any blood-thinning medications.

Quantity: 10 oz
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 2 ½ oz - dried willow bark, broken up into small pieces
- [ ] 12 ½ fluid oz - 100 proof alcohol (50%), such as vodka
---
## Directions
Grind the bark pieces into shreds using an electric herb grinder. Put the shredded bark in a clean 16 oz jar with the alcohol, secure the lid, and shake well.

Label well and let sit in a cool, dark place for 4 weeks. Shake occasionally, and strain well with a fine mesh. Store in a 16 oz jar for up to 5 years.
