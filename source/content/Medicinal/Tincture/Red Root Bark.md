> [!info] Dosage
> Take 5-10 drops 3-5 times a day. Best mixed with other tinctures.

>[!warning] Caution
>The root bark of red root is very trying and astringent. To avoid dehydration, constipation, or other complications from taking strong astringents, add a moistening herb like licorice root. Also, take only small doses of this herb at a time: 5 drops when used alone, or 10% of a formula.

Quantity: 8-10 oz

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 5 oz - fresh red root bark
- [ ] 5 oz - 190 proof alcohol (95 percent), such as everclear
- [ ] 4 fluid oz - water
- [ ] 1 fluid oz - vegetable glycerin
---
## Directions
Put the bark into a 16 oz jar and pour in the alcohol, water, and glycerin. Put the lid on and give it a good shake. 

Label well, making sure to include the added vegetable glycerin (i.e. 50 percent alcohol, 10 percent glycerin). Let sit in a cool, dark place, shaking occasionally for 4 weeks. The tincture should start to tun red within the first day. 

When it is ready to strain, use cheesecloth to strain out the chunks of root bark and return to the jar. Store in a cool, dark place for up to 5 years.
