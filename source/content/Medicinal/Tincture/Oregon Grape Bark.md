> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops 1-10 times a day, depending on application.

> [!warning] Caution
> Only take on a short-term basis, around 6 weeks at a time. Don't exceed more than 3 dropperfuls of tincture in one dose, and take that dose up to 8 times a day, only for acute cases. Watch for headaches or abdominal sensations, which are indications that your body has had too much.

Quantity: 4-5 oz

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1.25 oz - fresh oregon grape stem bark
- [ ] 3 oz - 190 proof (95%) such as everclear
- [ ] 2 fluid oz - water
---
## Directions
Put the bark in a clean 8 oz jar. Pour the alcohol and water over the bark. Seal the jar with a lid, and leave in a cool, dark place for 4 weeks. Shake occasionally. Strain through a cheesecloth and store for up to 5 years.
