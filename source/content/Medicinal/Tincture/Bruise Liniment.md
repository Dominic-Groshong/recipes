> [!info] Dosage
> Spray on bruised area several times a day, rubbing vigorously with your hand.

Quantity: 1 oz
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 tbsp. - [[_Tincture chart|goldenrod leaf or flower tincture]]
- [ ] 1 tbsp. - [[_Tincture chart|st. john's wort flower tincture]]
---
## Directions
Pour both tinctures into a 1 oz amber spray bottle, strain well to ensure there are no pieces that could clog the sprayer. The liniment will keep for up to 5 years.
