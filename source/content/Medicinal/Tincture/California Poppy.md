> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops under the tongue before bed.

> [!Warning] Caution 
> Do not take with pharmaceutical sedative medications. The combined effect can inhibit breathing.

Quantity: 10 oz
## Ingredients  
- [ ] 2.5 oz - fresh poppy (whole plant in flower)
- [ ] 7.5 fluid oz - 190 proof alcohol (95%), such as everclear
---
## Directions
Chop the plant into small pieces. Add the parts and alcohol into a blender until it is a chunky pulp, 1-2 minutes.  Pour the mixture into a 16 oz jar, secure the lid, let sit for 4 weeks in a cool dark place. 

Strain the mixture through fine-mesh and store tincture in jar in a cool dark place. Dispense into smaller dropper bottles as needed. Tincture will keep for up to 5 years.
