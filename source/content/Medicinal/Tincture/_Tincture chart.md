## Ratios
Use oz for measuring. Multiply the total oz of plant material by the second number in the ratio, that is the amount of "menstruum" you need (aka alcohol) you let the plant soak in to macerate.
## Maceration
Let sit for four weeks, shake occasionally. If you let sit for longer then 1 month, check for mold.
## Double maceration
Use two batches of herbs, first make a regular tincture, let it macerate, and strain it. Then add new batch of plant material to the previous batch, and let it sit for another four weeks.
