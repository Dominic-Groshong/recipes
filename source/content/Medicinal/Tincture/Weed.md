## Ingredients 
- [ ] 3.5 g - Marijuana flower
- [ ] 3 fl oz (88 ml) - Everclear
	- Using one bottle of everclear (750ml) makes 8.5 batches of tincture. Meaning you'll want 30 grams of flower.
	- A mason jar holds one bottle of everclear and 30 grams of flower perfectly
---
## Directions
1. Decarb your cannabis
	- Grid the flower, and place into a mason jar and tighten the lid finger tight. 
	- Place the jar in the instapot, on top of a wire rack. 
	- Fill the instapot with water, until the mason jar is 1/2-3/4 of the way covered with water.
	- Cook on high pressure for 40 minutes.
	- Manually release the pressure
	- Allow jars to cool.
2. Freeze the alcohol and cannabis in separate mason jars overnight. This makes the trichomes more brittle and will help the tincture taste less like plant matter.
3. Mix the alcohol and bud in one mason jar. Seal and shake it for one minute.
4. Place the mason jar mixture back in the freezer for five minutes to ensure contents stay cold and frozen. 
5. Repeat the shaking of step 3 and step 4 two more times. 
6. Strain the mixture through a filter of your choice, such as cheese cloth or coffee filter, to separate plant matter.
