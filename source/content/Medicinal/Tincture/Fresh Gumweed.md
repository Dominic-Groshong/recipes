> [!info] Dosage
> Take 3-5 drops ever 1-2 hours for acute cough; take no more then 10 drops in one dose. 

>[!warning] Caution
>Gumweed irritates the kidneys in large doses and should only be taken in small doses for short periods of time.

Quantity: 4 oz
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 cup - fresh gumweed buds
- [ ] 4-6 fluid oz - 75% alcohol (3 parts 190 proof and 1 part water)
---
## Directions
Chop the buds carefully, they are sticky; clean hands, knife, and cutting board with alcohol to remove resin.

Put the buds into a 8 oz jar, packed loosely. Pour alcohol over the buds until it just covers them. Seal the jar and label. Let sit in a cool, dark place; leave to macerate for 4 weeks, shaking occasionally. Strain through cheesecloth into a glass jar and store for no more than 5 years.
