> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops 3-5 times a day

> [!warning] Caution
> Don't use usnea internally during pregnancy. Usnea and other lichen tinctures can cause GI irritation in large doses, so it's best to take with about a half oz of water.

Quantity:  20 oz
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 2 oz - dried usnea lichen
- [ ] 20 fluid oz - water
- [ ] 10 fluid oz - 190 proof alcohol (95%), such as everclear
---
## Directions
Grind the lichen or chop finely to expose the white inner cortex, which is the part containing polysaccharides. 

Combine the lichen and water in a large pot and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce to low and simmer for 1 hour with the lid off. During this time, half the water should evaporate.

The liquid should be cloudy, and the lichen will be gloopy. Let this mixture cool enough to pour into a 32 oz jar. Add the alcohol while the mixture is warm but not hot. Seal with a lid. Wait an hour for it to cool, then shake. 

Let sit in a cool, dark place for 4 weeks and then strain. Store for up to 5 years.
