> [!info] Dosage
> Take small doses of about 10 drops as needed.

> [!Warning] Caution 
> Blackberry root is drying, and can cause constipation if taken when not needed, or in too large of a dose.

Quantity: 5 oz 

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 2.5 oz - fresh blackberry root, cleaned
- [ ] 4.5 fluid oz - 100 proof alcohol (50%), such as vodka
- [ ] 0.5 fluid oz - vegetable glycerin
---
## Directions
Use clippers to cut the clean root into the smallest pieces you can (1/4 in or smaller). 

Add the root pieces to a clean 8 oz jar and pour alcohol and glycerin over the pieces. Secure the lid on the jar and let sit for 4 weeks in a cool, dark place.

After 4 weeks, strain the tincture through cheesecloth and store in an 8 oz jar. The tincture will keep for 5 years. Dispense into 1 oz dropper bottle to carry/use.
