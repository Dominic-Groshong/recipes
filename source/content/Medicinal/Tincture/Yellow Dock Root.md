> [!info] Dosage
> Take 10-20 drops 2-3 times a day when symptoms are present.

>[!warning] Caution
>Yellow dock leaf contains oxalates that can damage the kidneys in large doses. Yellow dock root can be drying and should be used with caution for dry or constipated individuals. Also, because it contains anthraquinones, taking too much could cause intestinal cramping and diarrhea.

Quantity: 8-10 oz
## Ingredients 
- [ ] 5 oz - fresh yellow dock root, finely chopped
- [ ] 5 fluid oz - 190 proof alcohol (95%), such as everclear
- [ ] 4 fluid oz - water
- [ ] 1 fluid oz - vegetable glycerin
---
## Directions
Put the chopped root into a 16 oz jar, and pour the alcohol, water, and glycerin into the jar. Put the lid on and shake well. Label and make note of added glycerin. 

Let sit in a cool, dark place, shaking occasionally for 4 weeks. Then use a cheesecloth to strain and return to the original jar. Store for up to 5 years.
