> [!info] Details
> Fir resin can be used as a salve or oil on cuts to prevent or treat minor infections. 

>[!warning] Caution
> Do not take tinctures made from resin internally, can cause kidney damage or joint pain. Even small doses of 10 drops can cause issues.

Quantity: 

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 cup - fresh fir tips
- [ ] ½ cup - sugar
- [ ] ½ cup - vodka or brandy
---
## Directions
Chop the fir tips and pack loosely into a 16 oz jar. Add sugar and alcohol to the jar, put on lid and shake vigorously until the sugar is dissolved.

Let sit for 4 weeks in a cool, dark place, shake occasionally. Strain using a cheese cloth and store for up to 5 years.
