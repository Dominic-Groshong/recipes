Quantity: 2-4 applications (~1 quart)

#ingredients 
- [ ] 1 cup - dried nettle leaf (broken up)
- [ ] 1 cup - dried horsetail shoots (broken up)
- [ ] Boiling water
---
## Directions
Put the nettles and horsetail in a quart sized jar and fill with boiling water. Cover with the lid and let sit for 1 hour or over night for mor potency. Strain through fine mesh, and store in jar. Use up to half the "tea" to rinse your clean hair, focusing on rubbing into the scalp. Don't rinse with water, instead letting hair dry with "tea" in it. Use remainder over the next few days, storing no longer than 4 days.
