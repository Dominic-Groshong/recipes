> [!info] Dosage
> Apply to cuts, scrapes, or rashes 2-3 times a day.

Quantity: Four 1 oz tins

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 3.5 oz - calendula infused oil 
- [ ] 3.5 oz - plantain leaf infused oil  
- [ ] 1 oz - beeswax pastilles
---
## Directions
Lay out paper towels or newspaper on the counter and arrange your salve tins on top, lids off, ready to be filled.

Put the oils and beeswax into an clean 16 oz jar, it will be hard to clean so dispose afterwards. Add a couple inches of water to a medium saucepan. Place the jar in the saucepan and adjust the water level to match the level of oil in the jar.

Put the pan on a burner over medium heat. As the water heats use a chopstick to mix until beeswax is melted. Should be 5-10 minutes.

Once melted, pour into tins. Let sit uncovered until salve has hardened. Put on lids and keep in a cool, dark place for up to 2 years.
