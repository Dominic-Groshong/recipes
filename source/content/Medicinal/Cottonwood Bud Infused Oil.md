> [!info] Dosage
> Rub the oil into hands, wrists, knees, and neck as needed, or use it as massage oil.

Quantity: 8 oz

#ingredients 
- [ ] 1 cup - fresh cottonwood buds
- [ ] 1 cup - organic extra-virgin olive oil (more as needed)
---
## Directions
Add the cottonwood buds to a clean 16 oz jar and pour the oil over the buds until they are covered. 

Add a couple of inches of water to the bottom of a slow cooker and set the jar of oil and buds inside, uncovered. Adjust the water level to ensure it meets the level of oil in the jar. Avoid getting water in the jar at any point in the process. Set the slow cooker to warm, and leave  the lid off. 

Let the jar sit in the slow cooker for 12-48 hours. The longer it sits, the stronger the oil will be. Monitor periodically and add more water as the water line drops.

It is ready when the oil smells fragrant. Strain the oil with a fine-mesh strainer into a dry 8 oz jar and seal with lid. Store in the fridge for 1-3 years.
