> [!info] Dosage
> Drink the whole thing

> [!Warning] Caution 
> Blackberry root is drying, and can cause constipation if taken when not needed, or in too large of a dose.

Quantity: 1 cup 

#ingredients 
- [ ] 1/2 cup - honey
- [ ] 1/2 cup - apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups - blackberries, fresh or frozen
OPTIONAL
- [ ] Soda water
- [ ] Ice
- [ ] Squeeze of lime or lemon
---
## Directions
In a 4 cup measuring jug, combine the honey and vinegar and stir until dissolved

Put the berries in a clean 16 oz jar and pour the honey/vinegar mixture over them. Let sit in the fridge for 1 week.

Strain through cheesecloth, squeezing the juice out of the berries, and transfer to another jar with a lid. Store in fridge for up to 1 year.
