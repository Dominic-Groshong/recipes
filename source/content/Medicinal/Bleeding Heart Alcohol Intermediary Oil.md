> [!info] Dosage
> Rub oil into affected area several times a day.


> [!Warning] Caution 
> Bleeding heart is toxic when used internally. For internal use, do not exceed 20 drops of the tincture in one dose, and stop taking immediately if any symptoms come up.
> 
> Not safe for pregnant women, children, or animals. Do not take with pharmaceutical sedative. Unknown if there are other pharmaceutical drug interactions.
>  
> **Symptoms:** extreme sedation, breathing difficulties, nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea.

Quantity: 8-10 oz

#ingredients 
- [ ] 2 oz - dried bleeding heart (whole plant), cut into small pieces
- [ ] 1.5 fluid oz - 190 proof alcohol (95%), such as everclear
- [ ] 10 fluid oz - organic extra-virgin olive oil
---
## Directions
Grind the bleeding heart pieces in a herb grinder until they reach the consistency of coarse sand.

Put the ground plant into a clean 16 oz jar and add alcohol, mix well. Seal the jar and let set at room temp for 24 hours. 

Preheat over to 170 degrees F, pour the jar into a blender, add the oil and blend on medium-low for 5 minutes. Pour into 9x9 glass baking dish and place in oven for 2-3 hours. 

Strain the mixture through a fine mesh lined with cheesecloth. After straining let the alcohol evaporate, cover with loose fabric and leave at room temp for several days. Pour into 8-16 oz jar, **label** and seal, keeps in the fridge for 2-3 years.
