> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops every hour while cramping. Start with smaller dose and work up. Stop using if you start feeling nauseous.

> [!Warning] Caution 
> Large doses of cramp bark may cause nausea and vomitting.

Quantity: 3 oz

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 1 fluid oz - [[_Tincture chart|cramp bark]] tincture
- [ ] 1 fluid oz - motherwort tincture
- [ ] 1 fluid oz - [[Dried Willow Bark|willow bark tincture]] 
---
## Directions
Mix all tinctures in a 4 oz amber bottle dropper. 
Store for **NO MORE** than 1 year.
