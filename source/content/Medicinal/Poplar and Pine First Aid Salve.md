> [!info] Dosage
> Rub a small amount of salve on a healing cut or sore joint 2 times a day.

Quantity: Makes eight 0.5 oz tins.

#ingredients 
- [ ] 3.5 fluid oz - [[Cottonwood Bud Infused Oil|cottonwood bud infused oil]]
- [ ] 0.8 oz - pine resin
- [ ] 0.5 oz - beeswax pastilles
---
## Directions
Add the oil, resin, and beeswax to an old, clean 16 oz jar. The jar is hard to clean so recycle it once transferred to salve tins. 

Place the jar, uncovered, in a medium saucepan. Add enough water to the pan to reach the level of oil in the jar. Put the pan on medium heat, bring the water to a simmer, and stir the mixture with a chopstick until everything is dissolved. This should take 5-10 minutes. During this time, take the lids off your salve tins and line them up on a paper towel. 

Once all the ingredients have liquefied, carefully remove the jar and pour the mixture into the tins. Leave the tins open on a level surface until they harden completely. 

Label and store the tins for 1-5 years. Check for rancidity by smell — rancid oils smell sour and pungent in an unpleasant way. Poplar and pine are preservatives, the salve wont go rancid as quickly as other oils.
