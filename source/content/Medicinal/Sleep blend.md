> [!info] Dosage
> Take 30-60 drops under the tongue before bed.

> [!Warning] Caution 
> Do not take with pharmaceutical sedative medications. The combined effect can inhibit breathing.

Quantity: 10 oz

## Ingredients 
- [ ] 4 Tsp. - [[California Poppy|california poppy tincture]]
- [ ] 4 Tsp. - [[_Tincture chart|lemon balm tincture]]
- [ ] 4 Tsp. - [[_Tincture chart|valerian root tincture]]
---
## Directions
Combine the tinctures in a 2 oz amber dropper bottle, and store upright in a cool, dry place. 

Will keep for up to 1 year; after that, grogginess will worsen as the valerian tincture ages.
